## Preparing for Influenza Season (Tableau)

### Overview
This project leverages data analytics and visualization to assist a medical staffing agency in planning for the annual influenza season. The primary goal is to identify trends and patterns that will help allocate medical personnel efficiently across the United States during peak flu periods.

### Objectives
- Analyze influenza seasonality across different states.
- Identify states with large vulnerable populations and prioritize them for staffing.
- Explore the relationship between flu shot rates and the severity of influenza cases.

### Data Sources
- **Influenza Deaths by Geography**: CDC
- **Population Data by Geography, Time, Age, and Gender**: US Census Bureau
- **Influenza Laboratory Test Results**: CDC FluView
- **Flu Shot Rates Survey**: CDC

### Key Questions
- When does influenza season start and end in each state?
- Which states have the highest concentrations of vulnerable populations?
- How do flu shot rates correlate with influenza severity?
- What staffing levels are required to address peak flu season needs?

### Deliverables
1. **Spatial Analysis**: Map showing the geographic distribution of influenza severity and vulnerable populations.
2. **Temporal Analysis**: Line graphs demonstrating influenza trends over time.
3. **Correlation Analysis**: Insights into the relationship between flu shot rates and influenza severity.

### Tableau Visualization

### Success Criteria
The project aims to:
- Provide actionable insights for optimal staffing allocation.
- Minimize instances of under- and overstaffing during peak influenza periods.
- Inform stakeholders, including hospitals and medical personnel, with clear and impactful data visualizations.
